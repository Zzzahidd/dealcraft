# Dealcraft — Simple User Guide & Feature Manual

Welcome to **Dealcraft**! If you opened the application and felt overwhelmed by all the buttons, boards, and lists, do not worry. This guide explains everything in simple words so you know exactly what the app does and how to use it right now.

---

## 1. What is Dealcraft in Simple Words?

Normally, companies use two or three different apps:
1. An app to track **sales deals and clients** (like HubSpot or Salesforce).
2. An app to manage **projects and tasks** (like Linear, Asana, or Trello).
3. A search tool to quickly find things.

**Dealcraft combines all of these into one calm, fast desktop app.**
You can track a sales deal with a client, assign tasks to your team to fulfill that deal, take meeting notes, and search everything instantly with your keyboard.

---

## 2. The 3 Main Pillars of Dealcraft

Think of Dealcraft as three connected systems:

1. **Sales CRM (Sales & Clients)**:
   Track who your target customers are (e.g., Apple, Disney, Stripe), how much money the deal is worth, who on your team is talking to them, and what stage the deal is in.

2. **Project & Task Workspace (Team Work)**:
   Organize your internal work into Projects (e.g. *Website Redesign*, *Mobile App*, *Marketing Campaign*). Each project has a Kanban board, spreadsheet table, calendar, timeline, and files.

3. **Spotlight Launcher (`Alt + Space`)**:
   Press `Alt + Space` at any time from anywhere on your computer to open a search bar. Type a client name, task title, or command and jump straight to it.

## 4. How to Actually Use Dealcraft (Step-by-Step)

Here are the 4 most common things you will do:

### Scenario A: Start Your Day (Check Your Work)
1. Click **Home** in the sidebar.
2. Look at the top cards:
   - **Open tasks** tells you how much work is left.
   - **Overdue** shows what needs immediate attention.
3. Scroll down to **My tasks** on the Home page:
   - Click the **Upcoming** tab to see what you should work on today.
   - Click any task to open its side drawer, read comments, or check off subtasks.

---

### Scenario B: Manage a Sales Deal
1. Click **Deals Pipeline** in the sidebar.
2. You will see columns representing sales stages (*New Logo*, *Upsell*, *Expansion*, *Renewal*, *Pilot*, *Co-Sell*, *Land & Expand*).
3. Click on any company card (e.g. **Disney** or **Apple**):
   - A side drawer opens from the right.
   - **Overview Tab**: See the assigned Account Executive and Deal Health Scorecards (*Business Fit*, *Technical Fit*, *Security & Compliance*).
   - **Pipeline & Deals Tab**: See how much revenue this deal represents and the estimated close date.
   - **Stakeholders Tab**: See key contacts (e.g. John Doe, VP of Engineering) with direct phone and email.
   - **Notes Tab**: Read meeting notes or type new ones.
   - **Add Task Button**: Click the **Add Task** button in the top right of the drawer to instantly schedule a follow-up task pre-filled with this client account!
4. If you advance a deal in real life, simply click and drag the card into the next column (for example, from *Pilot* to *Expansion*).
5. **Create a New Deal / Account**: Click the **New Deal** button at the top right of the Pipeline page (or **New Company** on the Companies page, or **+ New** in the top navigation bar). A clean centered popup lets you enter the company name, website, pipeline value, and account executive. You can cancel or close it at any time with **Cancel**, the **X** button, or the **Esc** key.
6. **Create a Task from CRM**: Click the **New Task** button at the top of either the Companies or Deals Pipeline page to quickly add a follow-up task.

---

### Scenario C: Manage a Team Project
1. Click **Projects** in the sidebar.
2. Click on **Website Redesign**.
3. At the top of the project, notice the tabs:
   - **Overview**: High-level milestones, team members, and status.
   - **Board**: A Kanban board with *Backlog*, *To Do*, *In Progress*, *Review*, and *Done*.
   - **Table**: A clean spreadsheet layout where you can sort by priority or deadline.
   - **Calendar**: Deadlines shown by day.
   - **Timeline**: A Gantt chart showing dependencies.
   - **Files**: All wireframes (.fig), specs (.pdf), and images attached to this project.
4. Click **New task** (top right) to add a task to this project.

---

### Scenario D: The Magic Keyboard Trick (Spotlight Launcher)
You don't need to click through menus to find things:
1. Press **`Alt + Space`** (or `Ctrl + Space`) on your keyboard.
2. A sleek search window pops open over your desktop.
3. Type anything:
   - Type `"Disney"` -> Press **Enter** to open Disney's CRM account.
   - Type `"Wireframe"` -> Press **Enter** to open the design task.
   - Type `"Roadmap"` -> Press **Enter** to open the Q4 Product Strategy note.
4. Press **`Esc`** at any time to close it.

## 5. How to Delete Anything in Dealcraft

Every item you create has clear, easy-to-find delete options:

- **Notes**:
  - Open any note -> Click the **Trash** icon in the top header bar, or click **Delete** in the bottom footer.
  - You can also click the quick **Trash** icon directly on any note card in the Notes grid.
- **Tasks & Subtasks**:
  - Open any task -> Click the **Trash** icon in the top header bar to delete the task.
  - In the subtask list inside a task, click the **Trash** icon next to any subtask to remove it.
  - When editing a task in the popup modal, a red **Delete task** button is available in the footer.
  - You can also right-click any task or press <kbd>Del</kbd> on your keyboard.
- **Companies & CRM Deals**:
  - Open the company drawer -> Click the **Trash** icon in the top header bar, or scroll to the bottom of the Overview tab and click **Delete Company Account**.
  - In the Companies table view, click the **Trash** icon on the right side of the row.
- **Projects**:
  - In the project header, click the **Trash** icon (or click the three dots `...` and choose **Delete project**).
  - In Project Settings, click **Delete project** in the bottom footer.
- **Teams & Members**:
  - Edit any team -> Click **Delete team** in the footer.
  - In Members -> Click the three dots `...` next to any member and select **Remove from workspace**.

All deletions include a confirmation prompt so you never lose anything by mistake.
