# Strict Rule: Zero Corner Radius (0px Border Radius)

## Architectural Principle
All elements across the entire website must have **0px corner radius** (`border-radius: 0px !important` / `rounded-none`).
- No rounded cards (`rounded-xl`, `rounded-2xl`, etc.).
- No rounded buttons (`rounded-full`, `rounded-md`, etc.).
- No rounded badges, containers, dropdowns, inputs, or video wrappers.
- Every element must follow a razor-sharp, machined, Swiss editorial architectural aesthetic with crisp 90-degree corners.
